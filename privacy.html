Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

NoteCalcu does not collect personal data for the developer, sell personal data, or use your calculations for advertising or tracking.

Data Storage

The app stores calculation content and preferences locally on your device so it can restore your worksheets and settings. Calculations work offline without an app account.

Deleting a worksheet removes it from local app storage. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data; it does not by itself delete data already stored in iCloud.

Optional iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, worksheet names and expression/result rows are sent to Apple's CloudKit service and stored in your iCloud private database to sync between your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer does not operate a server for your worksheets or have access to your private CloudKit records.

Column widths and other interface preferences remain on the device. Disabling sync stops further synchronization and keeps your local worksheets; it does not erase copies already stored in iCloud. You can manage the app's iCloud storage using Apple's iCloud settings. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by its privacy policy and iCloud terms.

Sharing

Worksheets are shared as JSON documents only when you choose the share action and a destination in the system share sheet. The selected app or recipient handles that exported copy under its own policies.

Analytics and Advertising

The app does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party login services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page.
