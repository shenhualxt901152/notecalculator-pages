Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

NoteCalcu does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data Storage

The app stores calculation content and preferences locally on your device so the app can restore your worksheets and settings. This data is not sent to the developer or to a third-party service.

Deleting a worksheet removes that worksheet from local app storage. Uninstalling the app deletes the app's locally stored calculation content and preferences from the device.

Analytics and Advertising

The app does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party login services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page.
